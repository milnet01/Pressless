This manual shows how to do each thing Pressless can do. Type in the
search box to show only the tasks holding those words.

== finding-help | Finding help
Help is in the bar at the top of every Pressless screen.

1. Click Help in the bar at the top of the screen.
picture help-link.png | The bar at the top of a Pressless screen, with Help after Files.
2. Type what you want to do in the search box.
3. Read the steps under the task that matches.
video searching.webm | Typing a word in the search box, and the tasks that do not hold it disappearing.

== setting-up | Setting up Pressless the first time
The first time Pressless starts, it takes you through setup one screen
at a time. You need an email address. Your site lives on GitHub, which
hosts it for free.

1. Read each screen and do what it says. Some steps happen on GitHub's own pages, and the screen says what to click there.
picture setup-welcome.png | The first setup screen, headed Set up Pressless, with a Next button.
2. Click Next to go on, or Back to return to the screen before.
3. Click What's happening on a screen to read what that step does.
4. On the last screen, click Go to your list.

If you close Pressless partway, it starts again where you left off.

== your-writing | Finding your way around
Your writing is the first screen, and the first link in the bar. It
lists everything on your site.

1. Click Your writing in the bar.
picture your-writing.png | The Your writing screen, with the lists Drafts, On your site, Your pages and Parts of every page.
2. Click a heading, such as Drafts or Your pages, to open its list.
3. Click a name in a list to open it.

Drafts are entries only on this computer. On your site lists the entries
you have published. Your pages are pages such as Home and About. Parts
of every page are the header, the footer and the menu.

== finishing-setup | Finishing setting up
After setup, the Your writing screen shows a list headed Finish setting
up. A tick marks each thing already done.

1. Click a line that is not ticked. Pressless opens the screen where it is done.
picture finish-setting-up.png | The Finish setting up list, with two lines ticked and three still to do.
2. Do it there, then click Your writing to see the list again.

== writing-an-entry | Writing a new entry
An entry is a dated piece of writing, like a blog post. Your journal
lists your entries, newest first.

1. Click Your writing in the bar.
2. Type the entry's name in the Title box.
3. Click New entry.
picture new-entry.png | The Title box holding the words Low tide, with the Start from list and the New entry button beside it.
4. Click under the title on the page and type your words. The page shows the entry as your site will show it.
video writing-an-entry.webm | Naming a new entry, clicking New entry, then typing words onto the page.

Pressless saves as you type, and says Saved at the top. The entry is a
draft, on this computer only, until you publish it.

== styling-words | Making words bold, italic or coloured, and adding a link
1. Open the entry and click the paragraph.
2. Select the words to change.
3. Click Bold or Italic.
picture style-buttons.png | The buttons Bold, Italic and Link, with the colour buttons under them.
4. For a link, click Link and type the address the words should open.
5. For a colour, click Your site's highlight or Your site's quieter ink. For any other colour, choose it beside Any colour, then click Use this colour.

== adding-a-paragraph | Adding a paragraph or a quotation to an entry
1. Open the entry.
2. Click the part the new one should follow. With nothing clicked, it goes at the end.
3. Beside Add, choose Paragraph or Quotation.
4. Click Add, then type its words on the page.

== adding-a-photograph | Adding a photograph or picture to an entry
1. Open the entry.
2. Click the part the photograph should follow.
3. Beside Add, choose Photograph, then click Add.
4. Click Add a photograph and choose the picture file on your computer.
picture photograph-part.png | An entry with a photograph of a harbour, and beside it the Photograph list and the Caption box.
5. Type a caption in the Caption box, if you want one.

A photograph you added before is in the Photograph list, so you can use
it again without choosing the file.

== moving-parts | Moving or removing part of an entry
1. Open the entry and click the paragraph, photograph or quotation.
2. Click Move up or Move down to move it.
3. Click Remove to take it out.

== writing-in-marks | Writing with marks instead of buttons
Marks are a few signs typed among your words. Two stars each side of a
word make it bold.

1. Open the entry and click Show me the marks.
picture marks.png | The box of an entry's words, with the cheat sheet open under it.
2. Type your words in the box, with marks where you want them.
3. Click Cheat sheet: how to style your words, under the box, to see every mark.
4. Click View editor to go back to writing on the page.

The cheat sheet's A page to print link opens it as a page of its own.

== categories-and-tags | Giving an entry categories and tags
1. Open the entry.
2. Type the categories in the Categories box, or the tags in the Tags box.

== entry-address | Changing an entry's address
The address is the last part of the entry's web address. Pressless makes
it from the title.

1. Open the entry.
2. Type the new address in the Address box.
3. Click Change address.

== publishing | Publishing: putting your writing on your site
Nothing reaches your site until you publish it.

1. Open the entry or page.
2. Click Press to site.
3. Pressless shows a box headed Before your site changes, listing the pages that will change.
picture publish-plan.png | The box headed Before your site changes, with the buttons Publish now and Not yet.
4. Click Publish now. Click Not yet to leave your site as it is.
video publishing.webm | Clicking Press to site, then Publish now, and the line Published appearing.

Pressless says Published when it is done. Your site shows the change
within a few minutes. View your site opens your site in a new tab.

== changing-published | Changing an entry that is already on your site
1. Click Your writing, then On your site, then the entry.
2. Change it. Your changes stay on this computer, and your site still shows the entry as it was.
3. Click Press to site to publish the changes.
4. To give the changes up instead, click Bin this proof.

== undoing | Undoing the last publish
Undo puts your site back as it was before your last publish. What you
wrote stays on this computer as a draft.

1. Click Your writing in the bar.
2. Click Undo the last press.

Pressless cannot undo your very first publish.

== throwing-away | Throwing an entry away
1. Open the entry.
2. Click Throw this draft away, under the entry. For an entry on your site, the button says Throw this entry away.
3. Click OK when Pressless asks.

The entry moves to the bin in your Pressless-data folder.

== using-a-template | Starting an entry from a template
A template is a ready-made start: a poem, a lyric with verses, an entry
around one photograph, or a plain journal entry.

1. Click Your writing in the bar.
2. Type the entry's name in the Title box.
3. Choose a template in the Start from list.
4. Click New entry.

== making-a-template | Making or changing your own template
1. Click Your writing in the bar.
2. Under Your templates, type a name in the Name box and click New template. To change one, click its name.
3. Type the title and the words every entry started from it should begin with.
4. Click Save.

Bin this template removes it. A template is never put on your site.

== journal | Turning the journal on or off
The journal is the dated part of a site, like a blog. With it off, your
site is pages only.

1. Click Your writing in the bar.
2. Click Turn the journal off, or Turn the journal on.

Turning it off takes your published entries off your site at your next
publish. Turning it on again brings them back.

== changing-a-page | Changing a page, such as Home or About
1. Click Your writing, then Your pages, then the page.
2. Click a heading, paragraph or any section on the page.
picture page-editor.png | The Home page open for changing, with the page on one side and the Add list on the other.
3. Type on the page to change words. The boxes beside the page change anything else about that section.
4. Click Press to site to publish the page.

== adding-a-page | Adding a new page
1. Click Your writing, then Your pages.
2. Type the page's name in the Page name box.
3. Click New page.
picture new-page.png | The Your pages list open, with the Page name box and the New page button.

Pressless opens the new page and adds a link to it in your menu.

== renaming-a-page | Renaming or removing a page
1. Open the page.
2. Click Rename this page and type its new name. The old address still reaches the page.
3. Or click Remove this page. It goes to the bin in your Pressless-data folder, and leaves your site and your menu.

The Home page cannot be renamed or removed.

== adding-a-section | Adding a section to a page: a heading, picture, button, video, music or download
1. Open the page.
2. Click the section the new one should follow. With nothing clicked, it goes at the end of the page.
3. Choose what to add in the Add list.
picture add-section.png | The Add list with every kind of section showing, from Paragraph and Heading down to Your own CSS.
4. Click Add.
5. Fill in the boxes that appear for it, such as Words that describe it for a picture.

A video, music or download section has an Upload a file button for a
file on your computer. The list also has columns, a row of pictures,
a picture slideshow, questions that fold open, a picture that enlarges,
a back to top link and a countdown.

== moving-a-section | Moving or removing a section of a page
1. Open the page and click the section.
2. Click Move up or Move down.
3. To put it in a column, choose the column in the Move to list and click Move.
4. Click Remove to take it out.

== changing-the-menu | Changing the menu
The menu is the row of links on every page of your site. Pressless calls
it Navigation.

1. Click Your writing, then Parts of every page, then Navigation.
picture menu.png | The menu's links, each with a Words box, a Goes to box, and the buttons Move up, Move down and Remove.
2. Change the words of a link in its Words box.
3. Click Move up or Move down to change the order, or Remove to take a link out.
4. Click Add a link for a new one.
5. Click Press to site to publish the menu.

== header-and-footer | Changing the header or footer
The header is the top of every page of your site, and the footer is the
bottom.

1. Click Your writing, then Parts of every page, then Header or Footer.
2. Change it as you would a page.
3. Beside Show it on, click a page's name to see the change on that page.
4. Click Press to site to publish it.

== page-code | Seeing or changing a page's code
For anyone who knows HTML.

1. Open the page, the header, the footer or Navigation.
2. Click Show me the code.
3. Click View editor to go back.

== site-look | Changing how your site looks: layout, colours and fonts
1. Click Look in the bar.
2. Choose a layout, colours, fonts, the text size, the page width or effects. The preview beside each choice shows it at once.
picture look.png | The Look screen, with the layouts and colours to choose from and a preview of the site beside them.
3. Click Save.
video changing-the-look.webm | Choosing another headings font on the Look screen, the preview changing to match, and clicking Save.

Your site shows the new look after you next publish. Each colour has a
light and a dark choice: a visitor whose computer is set to dark sees
the dark one.

== files | Seeing or removing the files you uploaded
Files are the songs, videos, PDFs, ZIP files and text files you have
uploaded for your pages.

1. Click Files in the bar.
2. The list shows each file, its size and the pages that use it.
3. Click Remove beside a file to move it to the bin.

You upload a file from a video, music or download section on a page.

== site-name | Changing your site's name, description, icon or sharing picture
1. Click Settings in the bar.
picture settings.png | The Settings screen, with boxes for the repository, the site's name and its description.
2. Type in the box for your site's name or its short description.
3. For the icon or the sharing picture, click Choose a picture.
4. Click the ? beside a box to read what it is for.
5. Click Check the repository again and save.

== own-domain | Using your own domain name
If you have bought a web address, such as example.com, your site can
appear at it. Publish your site once first.

1. Click Settings, then Use your own domain name.
2. Type the name you bought and click Next.
picture own-domain.png | The first step of Your own domain name, with a box for the name and a Next button.
3. Follow each screen. Pressless shows what to enter where you bought the name.

You can close Pressless between steps. Settings, then Use your own
domain name, opens the step you had reached.

== visitor-numbers | Turning on visitor numbers
Pressless can show how many people read your site, by reading the
numbers from Google Analytics. It is optional.

1. Click Settings, then the line about seeing how many people read your site.
2. Click Sign in with Google and sign in on Google's page.
3. Choose the site Pressless should show numbers for, and click Use this site.

Turn off visitor numbers, on the same screen, stops it.

== who-is-reading | Seeing who reads your site
1. Click Your writing in the bar.
2. Under Who is reading, click the link.
picture who-is-reading.png | The Who is reading screen, with a count of people, a chart of people each day and a table of countries.
3. Click Last 7 days, Last 4 weeks or Last 12 months.

The screen shows how many people read your site, where they are, how
they found you and what they read.

== like-buttons | Adding Like and Dislike buttons under your entries
The counts live in your own free Firebase database, from Google.

1. Click Settings, then Like and Dislike buttons under your entries, if you want them.
2. Follow the steps on the screen to make the database.
3. Paste the firebaseConfig text into the box and click Save and check.

== youtube | Showing your YouTube channel's figures
1. Click Settings, then Your YouTube channel's public figures, if you want them.
2. Follow the steps on the screen to make a key.
3. Type your channel's address, paste the key and click Save and check.

== publishing-key | Signing in to GitHub again, or changing your publishing key
1. Click Settings in the bar.
2. Click Sign in to GitHub again, and follow the screen. Or paste a new key in the Your publishing key box.
3. Click Check the repository again and save.

Leave the key box empty to keep the key Pressless already has.

== more-sites | Looking after more than one site
1. Click Sites in the bar.
picture sites.png | The Sites screen, listing one site marked open now, with the Start a new site button.
2. Click Start a new site to make another one. Pressless opens again and takes you through setup for it.
3. Click Open beside a site to work on that one.
4. Click Remove beside a site to take it off this computer. Pressless saves it as one file first, and its site stays on the web.

Opening another site restarts Pressless in a new tab, so finish what
you are writing first.

== saving-a-site | Saving a whole site to one file, and opening it again
The file holds the site's writing, photographs and settings. It never
holds the publishing key.

1. Click Sites in the bar.
2. Click Save this site to a file. Your browser saves the file.
3. To bring a site back, click Open a site file and choose the file.
4. Open Settings for that site and enter the publishing key once.

== moving-computer | Moving Pressless to a new computer
1. Click Sites in the bar.
2. Click Move Pressless to a new computer.
picture move-computer.png | The box headed Move Pressless to a new computer, at its first step, Save each site to a file.
3. Follow its steps with Next: save each site to a file, take the files to the new computer, and open each file there.

== backups | Backups, and choosing where they are kept
Each day Pressless saves every site to a file by itself, and keeps the
most recent ones. They go to the Pressless-backups folder beside the
program.

1. Click Sites in the bar.
2. Under Daily backups, paste the whole address of another folder, such as one on a USB stick or in a cloud folder.
3. Click Use this folder.

A backup opens with Open a site file, like any saved site.

== bringing-in-wordpress | Bringing in a WordPress site
A site that holds nothing yet offers this, at the end of setup.

1. Click Bring it into Pressless.
2. In WordPress, choose Tools, then Export, then All content, and click Download Export File.
3. In Pressless, click Choose the export file and choose that file.
4. Follow each screen with Next. Pressless copies your photographs and files from your old site, and lists any it could not copy.
5. Choose a look, because a WordPress theme does not come across.

Posts, pages, the menu, comments, photographs and files come across.
Nothing is on the web until you publish.

== bringing-in-a-folder | Bringing in a site that is a folder of web pages
A site that holds nothing yet offers this, at the end of setup.

1. Click Bring that in instead.
2. Click Choose the folder and choose your site's folder. If it is in a ZIP file, click Choose a ZIP file.
3. Click Next. Pressless shows what will come across and what will not.

The site keeps its look and its addresses.

== theme | Changing how Pressless itself looks: light, dark or high contrast
1. Click More in the bar.
picture more.png | More open, showing Suggest or report a problem, Usage reports, About, and the Theme and Text size lists.
2. Choose a theme in the Theme list. Follow my computer uses light or dark as your computer is set.

This changes Pressless on your screen only, never your site.

== text-size | Making Pressless's text larger
1. Click More in the bar.
2. Choose Large, Larger or Largest in the Text size list.

== updating | Updating Pressless
When a new version is out, the Your writing screen says so.

1. Click Update now. Pressless installs it and opens again by itself.
2. Or click Later, or Skip this version.

Your writing stays where it is. To stop Pressless looking for new
versions, click Stop looking, low on the Your writing screen.

== jpeg-xl | Using JPEG XL photographs
JPEG XL is a kind of photograph file. Pressless needs one download
before it can use them.

1. Click Your writing in the bar.
2. Click Add JPEG XL support, low on the screen.

== shortcuts | Putting Pressless in the menu or on the desktop
1. Click Settings in the bar.
2. Tick the box for the menu, the desktop, or both. Untick one to take it away.
3. Click Save these.

This works when Pressless runs from the file you downloaded.

== ai-helper | Letting an AI app help
An AI app on your computer, such as Claude, can write and edit your
site through Pressless. It is off until you switch it on.

1. Click Your writing in the bar, then AI helper, low on the screen.
2. Click Switch on.
picture ai-helper.png | The top of the AI helper screen, saying Off: no AI app can connect, with the Switch on button.
3. For Claude Desktop, click Connect Claude Desktop, then quit Claude Desktop fully and start it again. For another app, follow the steps on the screen.

Nothing is published, undone or put in the bin until you click Yes in
Pressless. The app cannot reach your keys, your settings or any page's
code. Pressless must be open while the app works.

== usage-reports | Turning usage reports on or off
Usage reports are anonymous: which screens are used, where setup stops
and what goes wrong. They never hold your writing or your site's address.

1. Click More in the bar, then Usage reports.
2. Click Turn usage reports on, or turn them off there.

The same screen lists every report sent.

== reporting-a-problem | Suggesting something or reporting a problem
1. Click More in the bar, then Suggest or report a problem.
2. Click Open the report on GitHub.
3. Answer the questions on GitHub's page and submit it.

A report is public. Leave out your site's address, your writing and your
publishing key.

== restarting | Restarting Pressless
1. Click Settings in the bar.
2. Click Restart Pressless, then Restart.

Every Pressless tab open stops working, and a new one opens.

== version | Finding which version you have
1. Click More in the bar, then About.
